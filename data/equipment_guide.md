# Fictional Equipment Guide

Document ID: EQUIPMENT-001
Effective date: 2026-01-01

Dry vans are enclosed trailers for freight that does not require temperature
control. Reefer trailers provide temperature control; the shipper must state
the target temperature and whether continuous or cycle operation is required.
Flatbeds carry freight that can be secured externally and require suitable
straps or chains. Equipment type alone does not establish that a carrier can
handle hazardous materials; hazmat capability must be checked separately.
