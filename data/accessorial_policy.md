# Fictional Accessorial Policy

Document ID: ACCESSORIAL-001
Effective date: 2026-01-01

Detention begins after two hours at the shipper when arrival and departure
timestamps are recorded. The fictional Northstar Freight contract pays $50
per hour of detention after that free-time period. Layover requires dispatch
approval and written evidence that the delivery appointment moved to the next
calendar day. A truck-order-not-used fee is not guaranteed and must be approved
by an authorized operator before it is promised.
