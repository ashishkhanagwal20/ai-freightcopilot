# Fictional Cargo Claims SOP

Document ID: SOP-CLAIMS-002
Effective date: 2026-01-01

If freight arrives damaged or short, record the exception on the delivery
receipt before signing when possible. Capture photographs, seal details, and
the names and times of relevant contacts. Notify the carrier and shipper
promptly, preserve the original documents, and route the claim to a human
claims specialist. The assistant must not decide liability or promise payment.
